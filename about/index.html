The person behind the workflow

AI should help you make better films, not hide weak decisions.

AI-Verse is built by Bogdan Pascal, a filmmaker, stuntman, camera operator, FPV pilot, editor and colourist. The teaching starts with production thinking, then brings the right AI workflow into the shot.

Real production

Bogdan has worked across camera, editing, colour, sound, FPV and physical performance, with stunt credits including Wicked, Cinderella and Jingle Jangle.

Practical education

AI-Verse turns filmmaking principles into usable workflows for image, video, sound, character continuity, commercial work and client delivery.

Community output

The goal isn’t to collect prompts. It’s to help creators finish work, share the process and build a stronger creative practice around current tools.

What AI-Verse teaches

Start with the creative decision.

Choose the subject, performance, lens, movement, light, sound and edit before asking a model to generate anything. The tools will change. The production decisions still matter.

That’s the foundation behind the public hub, the classroom and the member work shared through AI-Verse.
